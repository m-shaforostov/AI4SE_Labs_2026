I found the prompt frameworks on the website provided in the assignment (frompting.com). I limited my search to the 
free options available on the platform, which narrowed the range of frameworks I could choose from.

I selected **STAR** because it helped me organise the prompt around the task context, the required actions, and the 
expected result. This suited my problem of converting natural-language questions into SQLite queries, where both the 
database structure and the output requirements needed to be clear.

I also chose **RACE** because it allowed me to provide relevant context, and specify what it 
should produce. Assigning the model the role of a data engineer and describing the required CSV format helped make the 
instructions more precise.

The platform suggested **CRISP** as the most suitable framework for my prompting problem while I was trying to use two 
other frameworks. I included it to compare the platform’s recommendation with the two frameworks I had selected myself.
